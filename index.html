How It Works

From first call to working appliance

A simple four-step process, so you always know what happens next.

  1. Contact us

    Call or WhatsApp us with the appliance type and a short description of the fault.

  2. Diagnosis

    Our technician inspects the appliance and identifies the cause of the problem.

  3. Clear quote

    You receive a proforma invoice listing the parts and work, with warranty notes.

  4. Fix & warranty

    We complete the work, test the appliance and hand it back with warranty cover.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Which appliances do you work on?

We install and service air conditioners, washing machines (including dryers), refrigerators, ovens and TVs.

How does the warranty work?

Our work carries a one-year warranty from installation, and products carry the warranty provided by the company. Warranty does not apply in the case of physical damage.

How do I get a price?

Contact us with the appliance and fault details. Once the problem is diagnosed we issue a proforma invoice showing the work and charges.

Do you offer credit or instalments?

Credit facility is not available. Payment details are provided on your invoice.
